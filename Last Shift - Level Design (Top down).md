## Player Movement and Gameplay Overview
The player will move on a 2d plane in 8 directions. They will navigate several hospital floors and bring needed items and equipment to needed beds in rooms. Each floor will have a top down view of itself and of the player and the objects in it. The player will constantly have to choose between which tasks to prioritize in the hospital. 

The player will only be able to take one item at a time. This is to force them to choose between the tasks that are at hand and decide how are they going to priotize their time and movement.
The player will also need to manage their resources (Energy, wakefullness) by going to vending machines and getting snacks for energy and coffee for wakefullness.

An example scenario: Suppose the player is carrying the medicine for patient A (Whose priority is low) and then they get a notification to bring medicine to paitent B (Who needs it more urgently) (I.e. bringing medicine to another patient who needs it immidietly). The player will have to choose between continuing to bring the medicine to patient A or putting the medicine on the floor and going to grab patient Bs medicine and bring it to them.
## Win and Lose Conditions
The player will win if they manage to make it to the end of the shift with energy and wakefullness remaining.
The player will lose if they get to 0 energy, and they will have to restart the shift upon loss.

## Level 0 — Ground Floor: General Outpatient Ward (Tutorial)
The player will start on the ground floor of the hospital, where the main storage room, vending machines, and other essential facilities are located.

This floor will contain only one patient who requires occasional treatment.

The player will learn how to:
- Move around the hospital.
- Pick up and deliver medical items.
- Read patient requests and notifications.
- Manage energy and wakefulness.
- Use vending machines to restore resources.

All required items will be located on the same floor as the patient, allowing players to become familiar with the controls without worrying about navigating multiple floors.

**Main challenge:** Learning the basic delivery and resource-management mechanics.

---
## Level 1 — First Floor: General Medical Ward (Easy)
The first floor will contain several patients who require more frequent treatment than the patient on the ground floor.

The player will be able to travel between floors via two ways: the stairs could be used to go up one floor at a time and the lift can be used to any floor.

Unlike the tutorial, most medical supplies will remain in the storage room on the ground floor. Players will need to travel between the two floors to complete deliveries.

This level will introduce:

- Navigation between floors using stairs or elevators.
- Multiple simultaneous patient requests.
- Different levels of task urgency.
- Planning efficient routes between patients and storage.

For example, two patients may request different medications at the same time. The player must decide which request to fulfill first, taking into consideration the patients' conditions and the time required to reach them.

**Main challenge:** Managing multiple patients while minimizing unnecessary travel between floors.

---

## Level 2 — Second Floor: Surgical Ward (Medium)
The second floor will contain patients recovering from surgery and others preparing for medical procedures.

These patients will require more specialized equipment and have stricter deadlines for receiving treatment.

The level will introduce:

- More urgent and time-sensitive requests.
- Tasks requiring coordination with doctors and other hospital staff.
- Medical equipment that must be delivered to specific rooms.
- Unexpected events that interrupt the player's planned route.

For example, a doctor may request equipment for a procedure while another patient urgently requires medication. Since the player can only carry one item at a time, they must decide which request takes priority.

Some doctors may also work on multiple floors, requiring the player to consider their availability when completing tasks.

**Main challenge:** Responding to unexpected events while coordinating deliveries and medical staff across multiple floors.

---

## Level 3 — Third Floor: Intensive Care Unit (Hard)
The final floor will be the Intensive Care Unit (ICU), where patients are in critical condition and require constant attention.

This floor will have the highest number of urgent requests, and mistakes or delays will have more serious consequences.

The level will combine all previously introduced mechanics while adding:

- Patients whose conditions can deteriorate rapidly.
- Multiple emergencies occurring simultaneously.
- Limited availability of certain medical equipment.
- Greater dependence on doctors and other staff members.
- More demanding energy and wakefulness management.

For example, a patient in the ICU may urgently require medical equipment while another patient on the surgical floor needs medication and a doctor on the general ward requests assistance.

The player will have to decide which tasks to prioritize, knowing that it may be impossible to complete every request on time.

**Main challenge:** Managing emergencies across the entire hospital while making difficult decisions under extreme time pressure.
