## 1. Market Research and Trends

An analysis of five games we found on Steam that are related to our vision suggests that there is demand for hospital-themed games. However, most of these games focus on managing an entire hospital rather than the experience of an individual nurse.

This presents an opportunity for our game to offer a different perspective within the genre.
## 2. Target Audience

Our target audience consists of players aged 12 and older who enjoy time-management games, simulation games, and fast-paced challenges.

The game will be designed for casual players, with short sessions lasting approximately 5–10 minutes.
## 3. Competitor Analysis

Our main competitors include games such as _Heart's Medicine_, _Project Hospital_, and _Two Point Hospital_.

These games focus on treating patients, managing hospital resources, or operating medical facilities. Our game will distinguish itself by focusing on a single nurse and the challenges of working under pressure, managing limited resources such as energy and wakefulness and juggling the many demanding tasks this job requires. 
## 4. Unique Selling Proposition

Unlike most hospital simulation games, Last Shift will focus on the experience of an individual nurse rather than managing an entire hospital.

Players will have to prioritize patients, perform treatment mini-games, and manage their limited time and energy throughout a demanding shift. They will have to constantly make decisions about what they want to prioritize: Their patients or their resources.
## 5. Business Model and Monetization

Our game will use a one-time purchase model.

We believe this model is appropriate because advertisements and microtransactions could interrupt the gameplay and negatively affect the player's experience.
## 6. Advertising and Distribution Strategy

Our advertising strategy will focus on social media, using short gameplay videos, trailers, and development logs to attract potential players.

The game will initially be distributed on Steam and itch.io, with the possibility of expanding to mobile platforms in the future.
## 7. LiveOps

Since Last Shift is a short single-player game, extensive LiveOps will not be necessary.

## 8. Meta-Game

The meta-game will focus on replayability through achievements, high scores, and unlockable content.

Players will be encouraged to replay shifts to improve their performance, complete optional challenges, and achieve better patient outcomes.

## 9. Analysis of Risks and Opportunities

**Risks:**

- The gameplay may become repetitive without enough variety.
- Balancing difficulty and time pressure may be challenging.
- The game may struggle to compete with established hospital-themed titles.

**Opportunities:**

- A unique focus on the experience of an individual nurse.
- Short gameplay sessions that appeal to casual players.
- Potential for additional levels, challenges, and mobile distribution.
## 10. Summary and Recommendations

Our research suggests that Last Shift has potential within the hospital and time-management game genres.

We recommend focusing on creating engaging treatment mini-games, balancing the difficulty of patient prioritization, and developing a short playable prototype to gather feedback before expanding the game.
